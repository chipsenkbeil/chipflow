## Instruments

- `$V/line.sh IMG W H X Y`: per-pixel hex dump.
- `$V/regdiff.sh A B GEOM`: count of differing pixels (any channel, exact) and their bbox.
- `$V/rows.sh IMG X Y W H THRESH`: ink row runs with per-run x bbox.
- `$V/cov.lua A.pgm B.pgm BG INK [prof|map]`: ink-coverage maps, centroids, column and row profiles.
- `$V/subshift.lua A.pgm B.pgm BG INK`: best fractional (0.05 px) translation of ChipFlow ink onto reference ink, with ink totals normalised so macOS emboldening does not bias the fit.

The reference is rendered with LCD subpixel AA, so its R, G and B channels
sample the glyph at -1/3, 0 and +1/3 px. Correctly placed text therefore
measures R -0.30, G 0.00, B +0.30 against ChipFlow's grayscale AA (H1 and nav
rows show exactly this). The **G channel is the geometry measurement**. The
chevron has no colour fringe in the reference (R = G = B).
