## 5. The fmt zero-padding follow-up — ruling and filing

The builder flagged: stopwatch count-up renders `0:04` (verifier observed
`0:03`) while KanbanFlow's chip shows `00:02`. Ruling: **genuine
KanbanFlow-parity deviation, out of KF-229 scope → filed as new defect
KF-231.**

- Evidence is direct: `.dev/kf-video-catalog/v9.md` line 19 (v9-e002):
  'the session appears as a running "00:02" chip in the top bar.'
- Root cause: `TimerUI.fmt` (static/app.js:2362) zero-pads minutes only when
  hours > 0 (`var mm = (h > 0 && m < 10 ? '0' : '') + m;`).
- It is pre-existing (not introduced by KF-209, not something the KF-229 fix
  overlooked) and KF-229's scope was the pill showing nothing at all — so it
  is correctly a separate defect, not a KF-229 FAIL.
- `fmt()` is shared by the pill, timer popup, tab title, and card live badge;
  direct video evidence exists only for the chip. KF-231 records this so the
  builder checks the other surfaces against KanbanFlow while fixing.
- Severity LOW (cosmetic). Filed in `.dev/kf-parity-defects.md` as
  **KF-231** [LOW | OPEN | Timer], Status OPEN.
