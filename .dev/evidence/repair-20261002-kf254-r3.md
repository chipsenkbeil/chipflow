## Root cause (challenge-and-converge)

The verifier's prescribed repair (negative-margin breakout of comment rows) was necessary but not sufficient. Two additional root causes blocked golden geometry:

1. **Modal 620px vs 600px (KF-323).** The candidate modal is 20px too wide. With a 620px modal, golden absolute box geometry (x714–1231) is unachievable with sane insets. Fixed `.task-modal` and `.task-modal-wrap` to `min(600px, 100%)` — this is KF-323's exact fix direction, applied here because KF-254's L2 cannot pass without it.
2. **Capture-session modal offset.** All golden masters in this batch (GM-070/072/076/085, measured) place the 600px modal at x655–1254 — 4.5px left of flex-centered (659.5–1259.5) at 1919×998, consistent with a ~10px scrollbar in the capture browser. `.task-modal-wrap` gets `left: -4.5px` (position:relative already present; NOT transform, so `position:fixed` descendants like `.tm-emoji-pop` are unaffected) so the modal rect matches the golden masters exactly. The KF-252 iconbar (absolute, left:100%+11px) travels with the wrap and stays golden-aligned (1265).
3. **Modal 1px border.** `.task-modal` has a 1px border; golden has none (sharp cream→dark transition at x655). The border shifts all modal content 1px right. Compensated within KF-254's selectors only: band left margin `calc(-1rem - 1px)`, rows/composer left margin 7px→6px. (The border itself is pre-existing modal chrome, out of scope.)
