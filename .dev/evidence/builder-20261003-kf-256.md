## Golden analysis (before coding)

The tracker cites GM-071. Two pieces of golden evidence exist and DISAGREE:

1. `.dev/evidence/golden-masters/task-detail-modal/GM-071-modal-open.png`
   (1919×998, DPR1): the subline row shows only TWO icons after
   "Created: Today" — subtask-list + comment bubble. Pixel scan of
   x 920–1150, y 85–115 finds no eye glyph.
2. `.dev/evidence/kf-drafts/v3-evidence/gm-v3-modal-golden-crop.png`
   (752×894, the wave-V3 verifier's cited evidence): shows THREE icons —
   subtask-list, comment bubble, AND eye.

The defect's fix direction explicitly says "add the three trailing icons
to the subline" (subtask-list icon, comment icon, eye icon), so the v3
crop governs. The v3 crop is a ~0.78× downscale; its icons measure
16×12 px (list, bubble) and 16×10 px (eye), vertically centered on the
subline (icon center y 78.5 vs text center 79.5), gaps ~8–9 px
icon-to-icon and ~16 px text-to-first-icon (at crop scale).

Icon artwork observed in the v3 crop (10× zoom):
- Icon 1: three small checkmarks + three horizontal #808080 lines.
- Icon 2: white-filled speech bubble, gray outline, three "..." dots,
  tail at bottom-left.
- Icon 3: almond/eye outline (light gray) with filled gray pupil.

All three are rendered unconditionally per the defect direction (no
golden evidence exists for the empty-subtask/empty-comment/unwatched
states; conditionality would be a new defect).
