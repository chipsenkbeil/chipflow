## Caveats

- Radius and insets are geometric readings from a raster capture, not
  computed CSS; colors are exact pixel values. The border reading
  ("none, shadow only") is what the raster shows at both edges and all
  four corners — no 1 px solid line exists anywhere on the card outline.
- The reference is the Colors tab of the "General" board's settings, at
  1920×1000, captured 2026-09-29. KanbanFlow settings chrome is global,
  so the board/tab choice does not change these values; a General-tab
  capture would show the same container with different inner content.
