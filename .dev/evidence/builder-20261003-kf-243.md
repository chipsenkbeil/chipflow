## Pixel analysis (golden GM-069-card-attributes.png, 1919x998 DPR1)

The tracker's "top-right of the title row" prose is imprecise; the pixels show:
- Avatar: 27px total diameter (25px white interior + 1px border). Border color
  follows the card theme (orange rgb(251,180,48) on orange card, cyan on cyan
  card, light purple on purple card) — matches each card's border color.
- Initials: dark brown/orange rgb(172,93,0), "CS" bbox 14x8px → ~11px bold.
- Horizontal: circle right edge 4px inside the card's inner right edge
  (x933-959 on the 515-965 card; margin-left:auto flush right).
- Vertical: on the icon card ("Build payment webhook handler") the avatar
  spans y150-176, i.e. it sits at the ICON ROW's height (icons at y153-166),
  NOT the title row (y136-147). On title-only cards ("Add export to CSV
  feature", "Add two-factor authentication") the avatar is vertically
  centered on the card (center 311 vs card center 310.5) because the title
  row stretches to the avatar's height.
